Rurality lookup points

How rurality is defined

We use the VA Office of Rural Health definition, which rates the census tract a place is in by its USDA RUCA code (Rural-Urban Commuting Area, 1 = metro core to 10 = isolated rural):

  • Urban: RUCA 1 or 1.1
  • Highly rural: RUCA 10
  • Rural: every other code
  • Insular: Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and Northern Mariana Islands

How it's calculated

  1. Every job location and every Census town or city center is matched to its 2020 census tract.
  2. Each tract's RUCA code is turned into a label with the rules above. These are the lookup points on this map.
  3. A job takes the label of the nearest lookup point in its own state, if one is within 15 miles. Otherwise, and for remote jobs, it's unknown.

Checked against VA's FY26 rural site codes, this gives the same rural-or-urban answer 96.8% of the time. Most differences are rural vs. highly rural.

Data sources

  • VA Office of Rural Health: rural definitions
  • USDA ERS: RUCA codes (2020 census tracts, updated July 2025)
  • Census Geocoder: coordinates to census tracts
  • Census 2026 Gazetteer: town and city center points
  • USAJOBS API: job locations
  • VA FY26 rural sites report (internal): used only to check the results
